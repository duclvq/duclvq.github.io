A2L ASSIST

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

A2L Assist does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • All information you enter (charge logs, training records, technician name and certificate numbers) is stored only on your device.
  • The app has no user accounts, analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • Local notifications for leak-check reminders are scheduled on your device and are never sent to a server.
  • PDF and CSV exports are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them.

Deleting the app removes all of its data.
