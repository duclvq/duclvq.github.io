This site is becoming a working lab notebook for the things I build and investigate.

## What I will share

Expect concise experiment reports, progress on pet projects, implementation notes,
and the occasional failure worth documenting. Each entry should leave behind
something useful: a result, a reusable technique, or a clearer next question.

The publishing system is intentionally simple. Every entry is a Markdown file in
GitHub, and GitHub Pages publishes it automatically.
